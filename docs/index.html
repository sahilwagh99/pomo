New: Ultra focus

For the days you go all in

Click the lightning button and drag in the apps that pull you away. Pomo powers up, with tall golden hair, fire in his eyes and a crackling aura, and those apps stay blocked, breaks included, until you power down.

New: a study coach

Know what to study next

Not sure which direction to take? Ask Pomo asks a few questions and builds your path: one goal, in small steps. You always know today's step.

  • Start small. "Just 10 minutes" gets you going, then Pomo asks if you'll keep going.
  • A study log in English. A few sentences a day, with gentle notes on your English.
  • App lock. Games you lock are put away while you focus.
New in 1.6

Pomo's room grows with you

Every focus hour, quest and Perfect day brings something new: a desk, plants, string lights, a trophy shelf, even a sleepy cat.

  • Moves straight in. The card at the end of your session tells you what's new.
  • Make it yours. Click any spot to swap what's there.
  • Keeps time with you. Sunrise to stars in the window, and lamps that glow at night.
For developers

Stay in focus while your agents work

A thought bubble while they work, a "!" when one needs you, and their exact questions and messages, ready to answer from Pomo.

  • Approve or deny requests, or reply when a turn ends.
  • Review their changes as your next focus session.
  • Take your break while they work on something long.

Privacy

Your focus is your business

Stays on your PC

Your history, ratings, tasks and parked thoughts live in a folder on your PC. No account, no server, no tracking.

Your key, your provider

Ask Pomo only sends something when you ask, and only to the AI provider you picked. Keys are encrypted for your Windows account.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub, and updates are checked against GitHub's checksum before Pomo installs them.

Ready when you are

Free for Windows 10 and 11. Pomo updates itself with one click.
